# ZoomRide SQL Project


## Project Overview
ZoomRide is a company that sells rides in cars and bikes, like Uber or Bolt. It works in 6 cities:
Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Nairobi, Accra and Kampala.
The data was messy. Some city names are spelled wrongly. Some trips are
saved two times. Some money amounts are missing.
I looked at the data, cleaned it, find the answers with SQL, and write a short message to the
manager.

---
## Question Answered
• Which city earns the most money?
• Which month do people ride the most?
• Which type of vehicle earns the most money?

## MESSAGE TO THE MANAGER
Based on the results, I suggest that ZoomRide invests in Lagos. 
Lagos earned the most revenue (₦218,890 from 93 completed trips), which is more than double Accra, the second city (₦92,640). 

I found two data problems. First, some city names were spelled differently (like "PH" for Port Harcourt), so one city was split into several groups. 
Second, 2 trips were saved twice, which would have counted revenue that was earned only once. 

Before a big decision, I would like to know why April 2026 was so weak and why 9 completed trips have no fare.
